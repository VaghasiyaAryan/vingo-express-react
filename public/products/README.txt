Put your real product photos in this folder.

Example:
  1. Save a photo here as:  mango.jpg
  2. Open lib/products.js, find the "Dehydrated Mango" product
  3. Change      image: null
     to          image: "/products/mango.jpg"

Recommended: 800x600px, JPG or WebP, under 200KB each.
Products left as `image: null` keep their generated illustration.
